      What is real, what is illustrative. Thrust, mass, diameter and length are PBS figures. The layout follows PBS's description of the engine: one axial compressor stage feeding one centrifugal stage, a combustion chamber, a single-stage axial turbine, a BLDC starter-generator, fuel-lubricated bearings and FADEC. One PBS distributor listing instead describes a four-stage axial compressor. PBS has not published a cutaway, so treat blade counts and internal proportions as representative.

      Station temperatures and pressures come from an ideal-gas cycle with an assumed overall pressure ratio of 5.5, 78% compressor and 85% turbine efficiency, and 1,150 K turbine inlet temperature. They are typical for this class of engine. PBS has not published them.
