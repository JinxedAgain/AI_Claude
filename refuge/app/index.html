Trading-day gate

8 trading days since your last request, at least 5 of them profitable by $50 or more, and no single day over 30% of total profit. Every account requests independently.

The $500 floor

Minimum payout request is $500 on every account size. Anything above it has to be covered dollar-for-dollar by balance above the minimum required — which is exactly what the target figures in the planner represent.

How to read a payout card

Blue is the most you can request on that payout. Below it is the balance you need to be sitting on first — min required balance + that payout — so the money leaves and the safety net stays whole. Fall short of that balance and the request is simply denied.
